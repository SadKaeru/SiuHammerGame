Hello!
This project contains more or less everything I know about UE5 so far.

Controls:

WASD – Move character

Mouse – Move camera

RMB – Attack with Hammer

E – Pick up items

G – Drop items

Hold G – Throw items (a line shows how strongly you will throw the item)

F – Start Minigame (near the pole with the red cube on top)

Crafting:
You can craft 3 different types of Hammers. Every Hammer has 3 unique skins depending on the stick used to craft it.

To craft a Hammer, you need 1 Ingot and 1 Stick.

Minigame:
In this project, I created a minigame where your goal is to destroy as many targets as you can by attacking or throwing your hammer in one minute.

To start the minigame, press F near the pole with the red cube on top.

Hammers Dmg & Durability:
Their damage and durability depend on the item you use.

They can also break if thrown—if their durability reaches 0, they will disappear. If you hold a hammer in your hand when its durability drops to 0, it will turn into a broken version with 0 damage and 0 durability, making it unusable for crafting.
